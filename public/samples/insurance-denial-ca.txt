REASON FOR DENIAL:

Our records do not show documentation of at least six (6) weeks of conservative treatment (such as physical therapy, anti-inflammatory medication, or activity modification) prior to advanced imaging, as required under the medical necessity criteria described in your Evidence of Coverage (EOC), Section 7.3, "Diagnostic Imaging Services."

YOUR RIGHT TO APPEAL:
You have the right to appeal this decision. To request an INTERNAL APPEAL (first-level grievance), you must submit your request in writing within one hundred eighty (180) days of the date of this letter. We will complete our review within thirty (30) days for pre-service claims.

If our internal appeal upholds this denial, or if we do not respond in time, you may request an INDEPENDENT MEDICAL REVIEW (IMR) through the California Department of Managed Health Care (DMHC). A request for IMR generally must be filed within one hundred eighty (180) days, and the DMHC may decide it within sixty (60) days (or sooner if your case is urgent). The IMR is conducted by independent physicians at no cost to you.

To file an appeal, write to the address above or call Member Services. Please include your claim number, any supporting medical records, and a letter from your treating physician explaining why the service is medically necessary.

You may also contact the DMHC Help Center at 1-888-466-2219 or www.dmhc.ca.gov.
